Shipping Calculator

The Shipping Calculator is a simple application designed to estimate
shipping costs based on package details.

The calculator can consider factors such as:

- Package weight
- Shipping distance
- Shipping method
- Delivery speed

The purpose of the application is to help users estimate shipping
charges before placing a shipment order.
